# Gujarat Site-Type Profiles

GeoReady uses configurable profiles rather than separate applications. Each profile selects weights, constraints, required layers, and explanation text while the score engine and map stay shared.

The initial Gujarat menu has eleven high-demand profiles: FMCG/retail store, convenience store, hospital/clinic, EV charging station, petrol/fuel station, warehouse/logistics hub, restaurant/café, pharmacy, bank/ATM, telecom tower, and solar installation.

All profiles remain decision support only until their Gujarat-specific layers and regulatory constraints are validated. India is a later coverage milestone, requiring a data-quality and calibration decision for each state/region.
